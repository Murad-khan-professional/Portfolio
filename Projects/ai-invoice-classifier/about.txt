This n8n workflow automates invoice classification using AI-powered document analysis. When invoices arrive via email, upload, or API trigger, the workflow extracts the document and sends it through an OCR and AI classification pipeline. The system identifies key invoice fields—vendor name, invoice number, date, line items, totals, and tax amounts. Based on configurable rules and AI analysis, invoices are categorized by type, department, or expense category. Classified invoices are routed to the appropriate folder in cloud storage and logged in a spreadsheet or database with all extracted metadata. The workflow handles multiple invoice formats and languages, flagging uncertain classifications for human review. This eliminates manual sorting and data entry, speeding up accounts payable processing significantly.

Tech Stack: n8n, OCR API, AI/ML Classification, Google Drive API, Google Sheets API, Email Integration
