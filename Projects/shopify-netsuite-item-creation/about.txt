This Zapier workflow automates the creation of inventory items in NetSuite whenever new products are added in Shopify. When a product is created or updated in Shopify, the workflow captures the product data including title, SKU, price, weight, inventory quantity, variants, images, and category. The workflow maps Shopify product fields to NetSuite's item schema—converting product types, tax codes, pricing tiers, and inventory locations. It handles variant-level sync so each Shopify variant becomes a separate item or sub-item in NetSuite with correct pricing and stock levels. Duplicate detection prevents creating items that already exist in NetSuite. Failed syncs are logged and retried. This eliminates the need for manual product entry across both platforms, ensuring the e-commerce storefront and ERP system stay in sync with accurate inventory and pricing data.

Tech Stack: Zapier, Shopify API, NetSuite API, JavaScript (data mapping, field transformation)
