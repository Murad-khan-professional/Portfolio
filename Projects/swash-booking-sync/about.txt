This Zapier workflow synchronizes booking data from the SWASH platform to external systems for unified scheduling management. When a new booking is created, modified, or cancelled in SWASH, the workflow captures the event via webhook or scheduled polling. It extracts booking details including customer information, service type, date/time, location, assigned staff, and payment status. The workflow transforms the data to match the target system's format and syncs it to Google Calendar for scheduling visibility, a spreadsheet for reporting, and optionally to accounting software for invoicing. Conflicts and overlapping bookings are flagged automatically. Cancellations trigger cleanup across all connected systems. This ensures all stakeholders see accurate, real-time booking information without manual cross-platform updates.

Tech Stack: Zapier, SWASH API/Webhooks, Google Calendar API, Google Sheets API, JavaScript (data transformation)
