This n8n workflow automates real-time notifications from Trello to Slack whenever a card is created or updated. The workflow uses Trello webhooks to detect card events—new cards, status changes, label updates, or due date modifications. When triggered, it extracts card details including title, description, assigned members, and the board/list context. The data is formatted into a clean Slack message with relevant fields and links back to the Trello card. The workflow posts to the appropriate Slack channel based on configurable rules, ensuring team members get instant visibility into project updates without checking Trello manually. Error handling logs failed notifications for retry. This keeps the team synchronized and eliminates the need to constantly monitor Trello boards.

Tech Stack: n8n, Trello Webhooks, Slack API, JavaScript (message formatting)
