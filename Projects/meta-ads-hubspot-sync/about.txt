This Zapier workflow automates lead capture from Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Ads directly into a CRM or database. When users submit a lead form on a Meta ad, the workflow receives the lead data via Meta's Lead Ads webhook in real-time. It extracts all form fields—name, email, phone, custom questions, and ad context including campaign name and ad set. The workflow validates and cleans the data, then pushes it to the destination system (CRM, Google Sheets, or database). Leads are tagged with the source campaign for attribution tracking. Duplicate detection prevents redundant entries. Optional instant notifications alert the sales team via Slack or email when high-value leads come in. This removes the delay of manual lead downloads and ensures immediate follow-up capability.

Tech Stack: Zapier, Meta Lead Ads Webhook, CRM API, Google Sheets API, Slack API (notifications)
