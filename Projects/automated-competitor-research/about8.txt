"This n8n workflow automates end-to-end competitor research triggered by a simple form submission. Users input competitor details, and the system starts scraping Meta Ad Library using Apify actors to pull active ad creatives. Videos and images are processed through separate sub-workflows for structured analysis. The workflow calls Jina AI to extract website content, messaging, and product positioning. For review data, it hits APIs from Reviews.io, Trustpilot, and TrustedShops to collect customer feedback and sentiment. All outputs are organized automatically—files go into labeled Google Drive folders, and structured data populates Google Sheets for easy comparison. Error handling ensures failed scrapes are logged without breaking the pipeline. One form submission kicks off the entire research process, delivering a full competitor breakdown without any manual work.
Tech Stack: n8n, Apify (Meta Ad Library scraper), Jina AI, Reviews.io API, Trustpilot API, TrustedShops API, Google Drive API, Google Sheets API"
