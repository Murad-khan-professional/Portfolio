# Automated Newsletter System

## Overview
This n8n workflow automates the entire newsletter email creation and distribution process. The workflow pulls content from various sources--blog posts, curated articles, product updates, or a content database. It assembles the newsletter by selecting relevant content, generating summaries using AI, and formatting everything into a professional email template. Dynamic personalization inserts subscriber names and custom sections based on preferences or segments. The workflow connects to the email marketing platform to send the newsletter to the appropriate subscriber lists.

## How It Works
The workflow aggregates content from multiple sources including blog posts, curated articles, and product updates, then uses AI to generate concise summaries for each piece. Content is assembled into a professionally formatted email template with dynamic personalization based on subscriber segments and preferences. The finished newsletter is distributed through the email marketing platform to targeted subscriber lists, automating the entire process from content curation to delivery.
