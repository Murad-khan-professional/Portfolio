This n8n workflow automates the entire newsletter email creation and distribution process. The workflow can be triggered on a schedule or manually, pulling content from various sources—blog posts, curated articles, product updates, or a content database. It assembles the newsletter by selecting relevant content, generating summaries using AI, and formatting everything into a professional email template. Dynamic personalization inserts subscriber names and custom sections based on preferences or segments. The workflow connects to the email marketing platform (Mailchimp, SendGrid, or similar) to send the newsletter to the appropriate subscriber lists. Performance metrics are logged after sending. Draft reviews can be routed for approval before final send. This transforms newsletter creation from a multi-hour manual task into an automated, consistent process.

Tech Stack: n8n, Email Marketing API (Mailchimp/SendGrid), AI/GPT (content summarization), Google Sheets (content source), HTML templating
